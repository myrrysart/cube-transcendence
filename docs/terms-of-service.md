## Accounts and acceptable use

Use an account you control, keep its password private and sign out on shared
computers. Do not impersonate others, harass players, submit unlawful material,
attack the service, bypass access controls or interfere with other players.
Follow the school's network and acceptable-use rules. Do not upload sensitive
personal information or use a password you use elsewhere.

## Playing and scoring

Players draw missing cube edges from a common reference. Multiplayer rooms support
up to four players. The host starts when members are ready; the server controls
the round's time window. The first accepted drawing is final. A missing submission
is treated as a timeout. Unsubmitted strokes can be lost on refresh or disconnect.

The shared engine scores accuracy and target coverage and publishes rankings after
the round closes. Higher scores rank first; elapsed time breaks score ties, and
identical score/time entries share a rank. Results are for educational play, with
no monetary prizes or promised qualification. Technical problems can interrupt a
round; the operator can investigate reports but no administrative appeal interface
or score-editing feature is provided.

## Drawings and visibility

You retain your rights in drawings you submit. You permit the project to store,
evaluate and display those drawings to the round's participants as needed to run
the game. Submitted drawings stay private while the round is active; final drawings,
feedback, scores and rankings are published to authorized participants afterward.
Browser-only creator/solo drawings are not uploaded by those tools.

## Availability and operator actions

This is an educational demonstration. It may be stopped, changed or reset by the
operator, and uninterrupted service or indefinite data preservation is not promised.
Persistent storage survives a normal restart; deliberate database deletion removes
stored data. There are no purchases or subscriptions.

The operator may stop a session or handle misuse directly on the host. The current
application has no admin dashboard, automated content moderation or account-ban
interface. Report misuse to the team rather than attempting to access another
player's account or records yourself.
